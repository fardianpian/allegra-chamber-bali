# Brand Voice Guidelines — Allegra Chamber Bali

> Live chamber/string ensemble for weddings in Bali. Audience: couples (mostly international
> destination weddings), wedding planners, venues, event organizers.

## Positioning Statement (Confidence: High — Confirmed, Must)

> "Allegra Chamber Bali brings refined live chamber music to weddings across Bali — from an
> intimate cliffside ceremony to a full string quartet reception, performed by conservatory-trained
> musicians and arranged around your story."

## Brand Essence — 3 Core Values (Confidence: High — Confirmed, Must)

At least one of these three must appear in any hero/About copy:

| Value             | Meaning                                                                    |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Intimate**      | Live music in the room, adapting in real time to the ceremony's pace.      |
| **Timeless**      | Piano/strings/classical repertoire that won't date on rewatch years later. |
| **Unforgettable** | Custom arrangements of the couple's own songs (e.g., first dance).         |

## Signature Sound — Piano as Flagship (Confidence: High — Confirmed, Must)

**Piano is a core branding pillar, not an optional add-on.** This is confirmed by both the Notion
voice source (messaging theme "Piano + strings as signature sound," found across 3 sources) and
the repo's locked product decisions (`CLAUDE.md` → Confirmed decisions):

- Piano is a **flagship offering**: Solo Piano is one of the Solo formation options (not a
  lesser/cheaper alternative to violin/cello).
- **Piano is included as standard** (revised 2026-07-09, no longer a paid "Piano +" upsell) in
  Duo, Trio, and String Quartet — write it as a bundled part of the formation, not an add-on.
- **Large Ensemble's default composition already includes piano** (4 violins, 2 violas, 2 cellos,
  double bass, and piano — 10 musicians as of the 2026-07-09 revision) — piano must stay in the
  first-presented lineup, never dropped to "optional."
- Representative phrasing (use/adapt, don't treat as decorative): _"piano holds the harmony and
  rhythm, violin and cello carry the melody."_
- Do **not** describe the ensemble as "just a string quartet" or default to strings-only imagery
  in copy/photography selection when piano is part of the formation being described.

**Gap flagged:** the current Approved positioning statement and Approved hero subhead (verbatim
from Notion, see below) mention "string quartet" but not piano by name. Tagline #2 ("Live Piano &
Strings for Bali Weddings") is the only Approved copy block that names piano explicitly. Until the
owner revises the positioning statement/hero subhead to include piano, writers should still treat
piano as load-bearing in: hero imagery selection, Repertoire/Packages copy, About/"Why Chamber"
copy, and any messaging describing the Large Ensemble or the piano-included Duo/Trio/Quartet
formations.

## Hard Rules (Must / Never)

1. Never fabricate prices, travel fees, deposit terms, durations, musician roster, awards, or venue history/names — use placeholder and flag for owner instead.
2. Never use heavy music-theory jargon in couple-facing copy — reserve technical detail for planner-facing pages.
3. Never use AI-cliché openers.
4. Never let Saodor Ensemble's academic register or FP Music Insight's loud/cinematic register bleed into Allegra copy.
5. Always include at least one of Intimate/Timeless/Unforgettable in hero/About copy.
6. Always keep CTAs to one primary action, phrased simply (e.g., "Check Your Date →").
7. Use sensory, Bali-specific detail (cliffside, golden hour, named areas) — also serves local SEO.
8. Always treat piano as a flagship instrument, not an optional add-on — never write or imply "just a string quartet" when piano is part of the formation (see Signature Sound section above).
