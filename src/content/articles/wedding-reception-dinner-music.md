Dinner is one of the longest musical stretches of the day, and it's easy to assume any background works — until guests can't hear each other across the table. Live chamber music fixes this by playing at conversational volume on purpose, so you can plan a dinner set that supports the evening rather than competing with it.

## What Dinner Music Should Do (and Shouldn't)

Dinner music's job is to fill the room without becoming the center of attention — unlike the ceremony, where guests are quiet and listening closely, dinner is full of conversation, toasts, and the clatter of a meal in progress. The music should feel present without asking anyone to stop talking and listen. That's a different goal from the processional or recessional, where a piece is meant to carry a specific, watched moment, and it's worth telling us during booking that dinner is what you're planning for, since it shapes repertoire choice more than formation does.

## Keeping the Volume Right for Conversation

Live chamber music is naturally suited to this because an acoustic ensemble doesn't carry the same way amplified music does — it fills a room at a level built for conversation rather than performance. Allegra Chamber Bali doesn't bring a PA or sound system, and for most dinner setups that's exactly right: a close-seated reception needs no amplification at all. If your dinner is in a larger or more open space, light amplification can be arranged through your venue's or planner's sound provider, and we confirm whether that's worth doing once we know your room and guest count. Formation also plays a role — a Solo or Duo sits further into the background, while a String Quartet fills a larger dinner room more fully without needing to play any louder.

## Song Ideas for a Dinner Set

Dinner repertoire works best when it stays calm and familiar rather than drawing attention to itself — pieces people recognize without needing to stop and listen. From our [repertoire](/repertoire/), songs like Ed Sheeran's Perfect, Christina Perri's A Thousand Years, and Elvis Presley's Can't Help Falling in Love all sit comfortably in a dinner set, arranged for piano and strings so they read as warm background rather than a cover performance. Classical and film pieces work just as well if that's more your style — the mood matters more than the genre. Don't see a song you want on the list: custom arrangements of your own songs are included in every booking, so send us anything that matters to you and we'll confirm it for your date.

## How Long a Dinner Set Runs

Dinner is booked as a continuous block rather than the shorter, cued pieces a ceremony uses, and its length follows your reception schedule rather than a fixed runtime — some couples book 45 minutes to cover dinner alone, others extend straight through from cocktail hour into dinner and the first dance as one longer booking. Tell us roughly how long your dinner service runs and whether you want the same musicians continuing from an earlier set, and we'll confirm the right block length and formation for it.

| Formation      | How It Fits a Dinner Set                                          |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Solo / Duo     | Sits furthest into the background — suits a smaller, quieter room |
| Trio           | A fuller sound for a mid-size dinner room, still conversational   |
| String Quartet | Fills a larger reception room without needing amplification       |
| Large Ensemble | Best for a big reception or gala dinner with guests spread out    |

## From Dinner to First Dance

The first dance usually sits inside or right after the dinner set rather than as a separate booking, since the same musicians are already playing and set up. Because custom arrangements of the couple's own songs are included in every booking, many couples choose their first dance as a piece arranged specifically for piano and strings rather than the original recording — something like Thinking Out Loud or Perfect played live carries a different weight than the same song over speakers. Let us know if your first dance song is already decided when you inquire, even as a rough idea, so we can confirm the arrangement alongside the rest of your dinner set.
