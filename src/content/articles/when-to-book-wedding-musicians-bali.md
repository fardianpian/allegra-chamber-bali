Couples planning a Bali wedding often assume live music can wait until other vendors are locked in — but popular dates and formations here fill up as far ahead as any other vendor category. Knowing the real timeline now means you can lock in your first-choice sound without scrambling later, whatever stage you're currently at.

## How Far in Advance Should You Book?

Reach out as soon as your date and venue are confirmed. Popular dates in Bali can book out 6 to 12 months ahead, especially during peak wedding season, so an early inquiry protects your date even before every other detail is settled. You don't need a finalized formation or guest list to start the conversation — confirming your date early secures the booking, and the exact formation, set list, and timing can follow once the rest of your planning takes shape. See [Packages](/packages/) for how the five formations compare while you're deciding.

## Why Peak Wedding Season in Bali Changes the Timeline

Peak wedding season concentrates demand into the same stretch of the calendar, so the same popular dates and formations get requested by many couples at once. That doesn't change what we offer — it changes how much runway you have to get your first choice. A date outside peak season generally leaves more room to decide later; a date inside it rewards reaching out sooner rather than waiting for every planning detail to be settled first.

## What Happens If You're Booking Last-Minute?

Reach out anyway — there's no fixed point where it officially becomes too late to ask. What's realistic depends on musician availability for your specific date, not a general rule, so the only way to know is to [contact us](/contact/) directly with your date and venue. We'll tell you plainly what's possible rather than leave you guessing.

## What Information You Need Ready Before You Reach Out

You don't need a finished plan to start the conversation — a few basics are enough to get a useful answer back:

- Your wedding date
- Your venue (confirmed, or shortlisted if you're still deciding)
- Rough guest count
- A formation preference, if you already have one — otherwise we can help you narrow it down

That's enough for us to confirm availability and follow up with a personalised quote.

## Does Booking Early Affect Repertoire or Custom Arrangement Choices?

Indirectly, yes. Booking early doesn't change what's included — [custom arrangements of your own songs](/journal/custom-wedding-music-arrangement/) are part of every booking regardless of timing — but confirming your song choices sooner gives more lead time for the arrangement itself, plus room for any revisions before your wedding day. If your date is already close, that just means arrangement timing becomes part of the conversation when you reach out, not a reason to hold off asking.
