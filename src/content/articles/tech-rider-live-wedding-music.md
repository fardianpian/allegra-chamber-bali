A venue that's never hosted a chamber ensemble often assumes it needs the same setup as a band or DJ — and that mismatch surfaces on the wedding day, not before it. A tech rider heads that off by spelling out exactly what a live ensemble needs, so you can brief your venue with confidence well ahead of time.

## What's Actually in a Tech Rider for a Chamber Ensemble (vs. a Band or DJ)

A chamber ensemble's tech rider covers different ground than one written for a band or DJ, who typically travel with a mixing console, monitors, and often their own PA system. Ours is simpler: a stage plot showing where each musician sits, an input list noting anything that needs a power source, and any space or shade requirements for the day. Most of what a chamber ensemble performs is acoustic or lightly amplified, not run through a full sound system, so the rider reflects that.

At Allegra Chamber Bali, a stage plot, input list, and power requirements are ready on request for every formation — message us on WhatsApp with your formation and venue and we'll send them over.

## Power Requirements by Formation Size

Power needs come from instrumentation, not headcount. Unamplified strings — violin, viola, cello — draw no power at all. Piano changes that: Duo, Trio, and String Quartet all include piano as standard, and depending on venue and setup, that typically means a digital piano or keyboard near a power outlet, sometimes paired with light amplification outdoors.

| Formation      | Musicians              | Instrumentation                                   | Typically needs power? |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Solo           | 1                      | Violin, cello, or piano                           | Only if piano/keyboard |
| Duo            | 3                      | Violin, cello, piano                              | Yes — piano            |
| Trio           | 4                      | Violin, viola, cello, piano                       | Yes — piano            |
| String Quartet | 5                      | 2 violins, viola, cello, piano                    | Yes — piano            |
| Large Ensemble | 10 (default, scalable) | 4 violins, 2 violas, 2 cellos, double bass, piano | Yes — piano            |

The exact power draw and outlet count depend on the specific instrument and any added amplification, so we confirm precise numbers as part of your tech rider once your formation and venue are set — this table is a starting point for planning, not a substitute for that document.

## Space and Floor Requirements for Setup

Every formation needs flat, stable floor space near the ceremony spot for each musician, their instrument, and a stand — not wherever guest seating happens to end. A piano or keyboard needs its own level surface, and any formation setting up under direct sun for an extended soundcheck and performance needs shade to protect both instruments and musicians.

Larger formations naturally need more room to seat everyone without crowding bows and instrument cases, and the stage plot for your specific booking lays out how much. Walk the exact setup area with your venue ahead of time, not just the guest seating layout — the two aren't always the same spot.

## Load-In Timing and Access

Our musicians arrive early enough that setup is finished before your first guest is seated, not still underway. The exact call time depends on travel distance to the venue and how straightforward site access is — a cliffside or beach ceremony with a longer walk-in for equipment needs more lead time than a ballroom with direct loading access. We confirm the specific timing with you once your venue and ceremony schedule are locked in.

## What Happens When a Venue Can't Meet a Requirement

Most restrictions can be worked around if we know about them in advance — a power outlet that's further from the ceremony spot than expected, a load-in window that's tighter than usual, or a venue sound policy that limits amplification. What doesn't work is discovering a mismatch on the wedding day itself.
